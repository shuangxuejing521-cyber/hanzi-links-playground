CJKRadicals.txt is copied verbatim from the official Unicode 18.0.0 UCD.
Unihan_kRSUnicode.txt preserves the source header and every kRSUnicode line
from Unihan_IRGSources.txt, with other property lines omitted.
character-radicals-audit.json is the relevant vocabulary subset with all
original kRSUnicode values, including simplified variants and multiple
classifications. See provenance.json for source URLs and SHA-256 hashes.

This is a dictionary lookup index using Kangxi 214 radical numbering.
It is not an etymological analysis or the modern Chinese 201-radical standard.
月 (74) and 肉 (130), and 阜 (170) and 邑 (163), remain distinct.
All source lookup assignments are retained; no historical images, semantic
functions, phonetics, or component-combination rules are inferred.
Existing illustrated families and combination cards are not altered.
